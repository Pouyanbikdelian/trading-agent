## POST

Last month I almost deleted half my trading system.

It's built as a committee of AI agents that debate every decision before the paper account moves. Early on the debates annoyed me. The agents kept disagreeing, and I read that as the system being broken. So I nearly collapsed them into one "smart" agent that would just decide.

I'm glad I didn't. When I went back through the logs, the trades that aged worst were the ones where every voice had agreed instantly. Unanimous felt safe. It usually meant they were all reading the same headline.

The disagreement was the product. One agent's job is basically to be wrong on purpose, to argue the other side so the rest have to defend the idea. That's not something I understood from papers. I had to watch it fail quietly first.

Still paper trading through Interactive Brokers. Still learning what these systems can actually do versus what the demos promise.

If you've built with multiple agents: did you design for consensus or for conflict?
