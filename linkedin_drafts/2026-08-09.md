## POST

Three parts of my trading system had never actually run.

Not "run and failed." Never run. For weeks the logs said the jobs fired, and they did fire. They just exited early every time and recorded that as a clean finish. I only caught it because I went looking for the output one evening and there wasn't any.

That's the part nobody warns you about with agentic tooling. It made me several times faster at building. It also made me several times faster at building things I never verified. When an agent writes a component, tests it, and tells you it works, the story is coherent, and coherent feels like true.

So I have a rule now. Every scheduled job has to prove it produced something, not that it tried. No timestamped artifact, it didn't happen. Boring plumbing. It has caught more problems than any clever idea I've had this year.

Still paper trading through Interactive Brokers. Still mostly learning where I fool myself.

What's the last thing you found had been quietly not working?
